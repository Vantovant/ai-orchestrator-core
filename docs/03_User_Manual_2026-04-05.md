## 1. Getting Started

### 1.1 Sign Up & Log In

1. Navigate to VantoOS in your browser.
2. Click **Sign Up** and enter your email and password.
3. Check your email for a verification link and click it.
4. Log in with your credentials.

> **Note:** VantoOS requires an invite code during the beta period. Enter your invite code on the sign-up screen.

### 1.2 First-Time Setup

After logging in for the first time:

1. **Complete the Executive Profile Wizard** (Settings → Profile Wizard) — this helps the AI understand your role, industry, and priorities.
2. **Connect Gmail** (Settings → Gmail Integration) — connect one or more Gmail accounts to enable the email module.
3. **Create your first project** (Projects → + New Project) — this unlocks the AI Partner and Knowledge Base.
4. **Set Executive Context** (Settings → Executive Context) — add key-value pairs like your role, current goals, and focus areas so the AI can personalise its advice.

### 1.3 Navigation

- **Desktop:** Use the sidebar on the left to navigate between modules.
- **Mobile:** Use the bottom navigation bar (Home, Plan, Email, Finance, More).
- **Command Bar:** Press `Ctrl+K` (or `⌘K` on Mac) anywhere to open the quick-action command bar.

## 3. Plan Hub

The Plan Hub is your unified planning workspace. Access it from the sidebar or bottom nav.

### 3.1 Today Tab

Your daily snapshot showing:
- Today's priorities (AI-ranked)
- Today's meetings
- Urgent reminders
- Quick-add buttons for tasks, meetings, and reminders

### 3.2 Tasks

**Creating a Task:**
1. Click **+ Add Task** or press the quick-add button.
2. Enter a title (required).
3. Set priority: Critical, High, Medium, or Low.
4. Optionally add: due date, project link, client/matter tag, description.
5. Click **Save**.

**Managing Tasks:**
- Click any task to open its detail drawer.
- Change status: To Do → In Progress → Done.
- Filter by priority, status, project, or search by title.
- Sort by priority, due date, or last modified.

### 3.3 Reminders

**Creating a Reminder:**
1. Click **+ Add Reminder**.
2. Enter a title and set the reminder date/time.
3. Optionally link to a project.
4. Click **Save**.

**Automatic Reminders:** A reminder is automatically created 1 hour before every meeting you create.

### 3.4 Meetings

**Creating a Meeting:**
1. Click **+ Add Meeting**.
2. Enter title, start time, and end time.
3. Optionally add: location, description, attendees, project link.
4. Click **Save**.

### 3.5 Notes

**Daily Notes:**
1. Select today's date (or any date).
2. Write your notes in the text area — they save automatically.
3. Use the **dictation** button (microphone icon) for voice-to-text input.

**AI Action Extraction:**
1. After writing notes, click **Extract Actions**.
2. The AI will identify tasks, reminders, and meetings mentioned in your notes.
3. Review the extracted items and click **Create** to add them — nothing is created without your confirmation.

### 3.6 Calendar

View your meetings, reminders, and task due dates on:
- **Day view** — hour-by-hour breakdown
- **Week view** — 7-day overview
- **Month view** — full month calendar

### 3.7 Secretary Mode

Enable Secretary Mode in Settings for AI-powered briefings:
- **Morning Briefing** — Start your day with an AI summary of what's ahead.
- **Pre-Meeting Prompts** — Get context and preparation notes before each meeting.
- **End-of-Day Review** — AI summarises what you accomplished and what needs attention tomorrow.

### 3.8 Voice Input

Click the microphone icon to dictate:
- Notes and meeting descriptions
- Natural language commands: "Add task: review contract by Friday" or "Remind me to call John at 3pm"
- The voice parser understands common command patterns.

## 4. Email

VantoOS provides a Superhuman-inspired email client that connects to your Gmail accounts.

### 4.1 Connecting Gmail

1. Go to **Settings → Gmail Integration**.
2. Click **Connect Gmail**.
3. Sign in with your Google account and grant VantoOS read access.
4. Your account appears in the settings list.
5. Click **Sync** to pull in your recent emails.

You can connect multiple Gmail accounts (e.g., Business and Personal).

### 4.2 Reading & Triaging Email

- **Navigate:** Use `J` (next) and `K` (previous) to move between emails, or click directly.
- **Open:** Press `Enter` or click to open an email.
- **Archive:** Press `E` to archive.
- **Snooze:** Press `S` to snooze until tomorrow 8AM.
- **Star:** Press `X` to star/unstar.
- **Waiting On:** Press `W` to mark as "waiting for reply" with a follow-up date.
- **Mark Unread:** Press `U` to toggle read/unread.

### 4.3 Smart Extract

When viewing an email, the Smart Extract panel shows:
- **Email Type** — Expense, invoice, subscription, travel, task, meeting, FYI, or other
- **AI Summary** — One-line summary of the email's purpose
- **Money Direction** — Whether money is coming in or going out (with amount, merchant, date)
- **Suggested Actions** — One-click buttons to create a finance entry, task, meeting, reminder, or note

> **Important:** All actions require your confirmation. VantoOS will never automatically create entries without your approval.

### 4.4 Views

| View | What It Shows |
|------|---------------|
| **Inbox** | All unarchived emails |
| **Snoozed** | Emails snoozed until a future date |
| **Waiting On** | Emails you're expecting a reply to |

### 4.5 Filters

- **Focus Mode** — Hides low-priority emails (FYI, spam)
- **Unread Only** — Shows only unread emails
- **Account Switcher** — Filter by specific Gmail account or view all

Your PhD-level strategic AI co-founder. Access it from any Project → AI Partner tab.

### 8.1 Available Modes

| Mode | What It Does |
|------|-------------|
| **Executive Brief** | Analyses your project situation and provides risks, opportunities, and recommended actions |

### 8.2 Partner Memory

Each project has its own "memory" that the AI uses for context:
- North Star (your project's ultimate goal)
- Target Customer
- Business Model
- Stage (idea → MVP → beta → live → scaling)
- Primary Constraint
- Weekly Focus

**Editing Memory:** Go to the Partner Memory panel and edit any field. The AI reads this every time you ask for advice.

### 8.3 Important Rules

- The AI will **never create tasks, meetings, or entries automatically**. You always confirm first.
- Funding recommendations **only cite verified sources** with URLs — no hallucinated programs.
- All your data is **redacted for PII** before reaching the AI.

## 10. Knowledge Base

Build a project-specific knowledge library that the AI uses for context.

### 10.1 Adding Knowledge (Text)

1. Open a project → **Knowledge** tab.
2. Click **+ Add**.
3. Enter a title, paste your content, and optionally add tags and a source URL.
4. Click **Add Knowledge Doc**.

### 10.2 Uploading Files

1. In the Add Knowledge modal, drag and drop a file (or click to browse).
2. Supported formats: **PDF, DOCX, TXT, MD, CSV, JSON, HTML**.
3. VantoOS automatically:
   - Stores the file securely (private, encrypted storage)
   - Extracts text from the file
   - Splits text into chunks for AI retrieval
4. If text extraction fails (e.g., scanned PDF), you'll see a message — you can paste the text manually.

### 10.3 Filters

| Filter | What It Shows |
|--------|---------------|
| **This Project** | Only documents assigned to the current project |
| **All Projects** | Documents from all your projects |
| **Global Only** | Documents not assigned to any specific project |

### 10.4 Smart Project Assignment

When adding a document:
1. Click **Smart Suggest** — the AI analyses the title and content.
2. It suggests which project the document best fits (with confidence %).
3. You can accept the suggestion or manually choose a different project.
4. Nothing is assigned without your confirmation.

### 10.5 Downloading Files

For uploaded documents, click the **Download** button (↓ icon) on the document card to get a signed download link.

### 10.6 How It Works with AI

When you ask the AI Partner for advice in a project, it automatically retrieves relevant knowledge from that project's documents. This means:
- Your AI partner has project-specific context.
- Documents from other projects are **not** mixed in unless you select "All Projects."
- Global documents are included only if you toggle that option.

## 12. Export & Import

### 12.1 Exporting Data

1. Go to **Settings → Export & Import**.
2. Choose what to export:
   - Individual entities: Tasks, Meetings, Reminders, Notes, Knowledge Docs
   - Full Workspace Bundle (ZIP of all data)
3. Choose format: CSV or JSON.
4. Click **Export** — a file downloads to your computer.

### 12.2 Importing Data

1. Go to **Settings → Export & Import**.
2. Choose the entity type to import.
3. Select a CSV or JSON file.
4. VantoOS automatically:
   - Detects duplicates using ID matching or content-based deduplication.
   - Updates existing records (upsert) instead of creating duplicates.
   - Preserves project links and metadata.

> **Tip:** Use Export → Import to sync data between devices, create backups, or transfer data between environments.

1. Open the extension side panel → Settings.

## 18. Troubleshooting

### Common Issues

| Issue | Solution |
|-------|----------|
| **Dashboard shows no data** | Click the refresh button. Make sure you have tasks, meetings, or reminders created. |
| **AI briefing not loading** | Check if you have AI credits remaining (Settings → AI). The AI needs at least some data to work with. |
| **Gmail sync failing** | Disconnect and reconnect your Gmail account in Settings. Ensure you've granted the required permissions. |
| **File upload failing** | Check file size (max 20MB). Ensure the file format is supported (PDF, DOCX, TXT, MD, CSV, JSON, HTML). |
| **Text extraction failed** | This can happen with scanned PDFs or complex layouts. You can paste the text manually in the content field. |
| **Export not downloading** | Check your browser's download settings. Try a different format (CSV vs. JSON). |
| **Import creating duplicates** | VantoOS uses deduplication by default. If you're seeing duplicates, the imported data may have different IDs or titles. |
| **Chrome extension not connecting** | Generate a new pairing code and try again. Codes expire after 10 minutes. |

### Data Safety

- **All your data is private** — Row Level Security ensures no other user can see your data.
- **Deletions are recoverable** — VantoOS uses soft-delete, so data can be restored.
- **AI never stores your data** — Text sent to AI models is not retained by the providers.
- **PII is always redacted** — SA ID numbers, phone numbers, and bank details are scrubbed before reaching AI.
